Minimizing energy & delivery time while ensuring high reliability in UAVs

Every drone mission trades battery energy against delivery time and the risk of failure. Flight speed is the one knob that controls all three. This tool models a multirotor's power, time and reliability, then finds the optimal feasible speed for your mission using three optimisation algorithms.

How it works

A quantitative, simulation-based model optimised under constraints.

Power

P(V) = P₀(1+3V²/U²) + Pᵢ·√(√(1+V⁴/4v₀⁴) − V²/2v₀²) + ½d₀ρsA·V³

Rotary-wing model of Zeng, Xu & Zhang (2019), scaled to the drone's mass.

Time & energy

T(V) = D / (V − w)    E(V) = P(V)·T(V)

Headwind w slows ground speed. Battery budget = 0.8 × hover power × published flight time.

Reliability (assumption)

R(V) = exp(−λ(V)·T)   λ(V) = λ₀(1 + k(V/Vmax)²)

No drone maker publishes failure rates, so λ₀ and k are illustrative (after Petritoli et al., 2018).

Optimisation

min {E, T}  s.t.  R ≥ R_min, E ≤ budget, V ≤ Vmax

Weighted sum J = w·E/E_min + (1−w)·T/T_min picks one speed from the Pareto front.
